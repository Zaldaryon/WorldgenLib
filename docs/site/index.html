WorldgenLib is infrastructure, not a terrain overhaul. It adds no blocks, items, biomes, or world shape by itself. A consumer mod chooses the hook points it needs and keeps ownership of its own algorithm.

A shared contract for worldgen authors

One generation pipeline. Many compatible mods.

Use the smallest seam that expresses the change. WorldgenLib orders registered hooks, carries the state needed by the current request, and reports the boundaries where a complete replacement would collide.

The useful boundary

Register where the algorithm changes.

Consumers can compose local effects without silently becoming the second owner of an entire vanilla generator. Equal orders remain stable, failures are isolated to the affected registration, and blocking takeovers are reported.

Compatibility

Know what the library promises.

WorldgenLib has server lifecycle evidence across Vintage Story 1.22.0 through 1.22.7. Its canonical parity checks cover the documented map-region and terrain probe boundaries.

That evidence does not claim byte-identical persisted saves, entity state, lighting, or every foreign Harmony or IL patch. Read the limits before pairing it with a full worldgen replacement.
